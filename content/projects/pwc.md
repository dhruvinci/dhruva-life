I joined PwC US in January 2018, straight out of my master's, and stayed till November 2021. Enterprise architecture for big clients - integrations, mostly Salesforce and MuleSoft, rationalising sprawling tool stacks, and the technology side of mergers and divestitures - working with offshore development teams to ship it.

It taught me how large systems really fit together, and how much of the work is getting people to agree on them.
