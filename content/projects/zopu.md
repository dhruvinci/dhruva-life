Zopu.ai (it started life as WideCanvas.ai) is my friend Roshan's startup. The idea: most people are bad at typing prompts, but anyone can sketch. So instead of a chat box you get an interactive board, a bit like Miro, where you draw your product, your architecture or your system, and Zopu builds from that.

I joined as founding AI engineer in January 2025 and built the entire drawing-to-output pipeline - the multi-model architecture behind it, optimising it, and hosting it on our own servers. We scaled to about 200,000 daily active users at the peak.

Working on how models read drawings is what pulled me into vision research in the first place. Roshan saw the harness I'd built and encouraged me to take it further into my own product and paper - which is how [Kakashi](/work/kakashi) happened.
