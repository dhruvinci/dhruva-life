I've been going to gigs since 2012 and the indie scene has given me some of the best nights of my life. Pushit TV started as an Instagram channel of footage from those nights - rehearsals, sets, the people behind them. [pushit.tv](https://www.pushit.tv) is its home: every tape gets a page, full sets are there to watch, and the bands and venues are all linked up.
