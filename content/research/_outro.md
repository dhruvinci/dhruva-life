## Kakashi.ai, the side project

[Kakashi](https://kakashi.ai) came out of this research and fed it back: crowdsourced data from 377 people across more than 250 gyms, coaches correcting what the model said, and a clearer view of the problems that actually matter.

## Code, models and data

Every thread has its own repo, with the numbers sourced and the negative results kept - [the index](https://github.com/dhruvinci/research) has the timeline. Models are public on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/0xdhruva). The MoveBench v0 seed is public in [moveharness](https://github.com/dhruvinci/moveharness/tree/main/data/v0); the other datasets are available on request.
