I walked into my first jiu-jitsu class the Monday after selling my startup, in December 2023, and blacked out at the end of it. I've been hooked since. I'm a guard player - most of my game is on my back, patient, waiting for the moment to turn it around.

## Fights

- **PKD** · 2025 · 5 fights, 3 wins, 2 losses. No nerves for the first time. Somebody said I was playing like Rahul Dravid.
- **Strangle** · Delhi · Dec 2024 · Won my first fight, lost the second fifteen minutes later.
- **ADCC India Nationals** · Delhi · May 2024 · My first competition. Lost on points, and didn't tap to the choke.

## Where I train

- **Institute of Jiu Jitsu**, Bangalore - where it started.
- **Flamingo Jiu Jitsu**, Bangalore - run by Debo, who cornered me at my first fight.
