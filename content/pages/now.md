Mid-research on a question that sounds simple and isn't: when two people are tangled together, whose arm is it? I've spent the last couple of months probing frozen models - Qwen3.8-27B, DINO, Laya - with small trained readouts, trying to get a model's sense of who's who to land on the right pixels. So far it mostly hasn't, and the controls show exactly why, which is its own kind of progress. [The details are here](/research/whose-arm-is-it).

Around that, I'm building towards a fast/slow vision stack - quick System 1 models for posture, pose and movement, slower VLM reasoning on top. [What's held up so far](/research/fast-and-slow).

[Kakashi](/work/kakashi) keeps going as a side project. More papers are in the pipeline.

Documenting the indie scene with [pushit.tv](/work/pushit).

Looking for people to do this with - a PhD, a lab, or a team building vision AI and robots. [Get in touch](/contact).
