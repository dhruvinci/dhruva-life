Mid-research on my own vision pipeline. I'm trying to marry System 1 and System 2 - the fast/slow idea from Kahneman's *Thinking, Fast and Slow* - for visual understanding. Classifiers, segmentation and pose are quick and reflexive; VLMs are slow and reason. I've been pairing Laya Vision with a small VLM and a fine-tuned Qwen, pulling the vision tower out and probing layers to see how much spatial information is actually in there. It's running on my other machine as I write this.

[Kakashi](/work/kakashi) keeps going as a side project. More papers are in the pipeline.

Documenting the indie scene with [pushit.tv](/work/pushit).

Looking for people to do this with - a PhD, a lab, or a team building vision AI and robots. [Get in touch](/contact).
