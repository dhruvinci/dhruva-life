Most of my photography is waiting. At jiu-jitsu and MMA competitions you wait for the right angle, the right timing, the right frame - a move about to land, a finish, the look on someone's face in the middle of a roll. Most of the time you miss it. When you get it, it's one of the best feelings there is.

- **Sony α7 IV** - the workhorse, for fights and anything that moves fast.
- **Fujifilm X100VI** - the one that's always with me.

I also document the indie music scene with [pushit.tv](https://www.pushit.tv) - rehearsals, sets, and the people behind them.
