# The larger story

The essays are small pictures of one life. This is the map of that life as it's told publicly, so a new piece can sit in the right place without narrating any of it. Use it to know what he's already said, what a callback would mean, and what an essay at a given date could and couldn't know. Don't paste it into posts.

Private context that explains more of it lives in `~/.claude/essay-private/context.md`.

## Threads

Each thread is something he keeps coming back to. A new essay usually pulls one of them, lightly.

- **Loving things until they become builds.** Jiu-jitsu → Kakashi → the paper. Music → the scene, records, pushit.tv. Drawings → Zopu → how models see. "The things I love don't stay hobbies for long."
- **The uncomfortable road, with nobody ahead to ask.** the-year-off, taking-the-leap ("I keep ending up on roads where there's nobody ahead of me to ask"), coming-home-to-build, six-hours-a-day, vibe-researching ("no lab, no advisor, no co-authors").
- **Seeds and long games.** coming-home-to-build ("Let's plant some seeds"), hire-the-hungry (the butterfly effect), when-to-stop-playing, a-sport-for-life ("See you at seventy"), shipping-kakashi ("This is going to take years. Good.").
- **Honesty over fluency.** The strongest intellectual thread, from crypto to research: when-to-stop-playing ("nicer stories are how you end up making the same mistake twice"), building-in-the-cold, fluent-is-not-faithful, legible-partiality, vibe-researching ("The AI can own the sentences. The human owns the truth."), chasing-what-is-true, leg-spaghetti.
- **The body as instrument and teacher.** six-hours-a-day, the-wall (composure is earned), a-sport-for-life, chess-in-four-dimensions, seeing-fast-and-slow ("a little of it has moved into the glance"). His own rolls are his ground truth.
- **Fast and slow.** watching-robots-box, analog ("I need the same balance myself"), seeing-fast-and-slow, research/fast-and-slow. Where his life and his research meet.
- **Positive-sum games.** sovereignty, the-scene, when-to-stop-playing ("My purpose in life is to play positive sum games").
- **Building yourself out of the story.** productization, handing-it-over, one-click (innovation that disappears), shipping-kakashi ("The coaches are the team").
- **The system vs thinking for yourself.** the-year-off (the answer key, "The fury."), coming-home-to-build (the underrated kid), hire-the-hungry ("CVs lie in both directions"), architect-in-the-loop, legible-partiality.
- **Family and home.** taking-the-leap, analog (his mother's scooter and knife, his dad's helmet, chair and amp), chasing-what-is-true ("My parents taught me to chase what is true and share it").
- **Music and the scene.** the-scene, chess-in-four-dimensions (flow at gigs), sovereignty (Dark Side of the Moon), analog (Fear of a Blank Planet), the music page.

## Essays that talk to each other

Callbacks are welcome when they're earned; one per essay is plenty.

- Fear over time: six-hours-a-day ("walk next to it") → a-sport-for-life (the Dune litany) → the-wall ("This time I slept fine.").
- Losing and refinding the love of code: the-year-off → architect-in-the-loop.
- Chess in 4D: chess-in-four-dimensions → a-coach-that-watches → a-machine-in-a-mans-world, where time becomes the research problem.
- The Tackett match: a-coach-that-watches → fluent-is-not-faithful ("That paragraph has followed me around ever since.").
- Bull-market honesty → research honesty: when-to-stop-playing / building-in-the-cold → fluent-is-not-faithful / chasing-what-is-true.
- Drawing → seeing: a-picture-and-a-thousand-prompts → the-trouble-with-drawings ("I catch myself reading about how models see anything at all") → a-coach-that-watches.
- Whose limb is it: research/whose-arm-is-it → seeing-fast-and-slow ("a binding problem") → leg-spaghetti ("Whose limb is that?"). "Let it be unsure" in leg-spaghetti echoes "room to say 'not sure'" in fluent-is-not-faithful.
- "Going to bed exhausted every day, knowing I gave it my all": the-year-off, echoed in shipping-kakashi.

## Open tensions (public)

Things the published essays haven't reconciled. Good essays live here, if he wants to go there.

- architect-in-the-loop ("I fired 3 of my developers") sits awkwardly next to hire-the-hungry ("give it something real to chew on… They'll outgrow you").
- watching-robots-box and the about page point at robots that work with people; there's no essay yet on what that would actually take.
- Drawing → seeing is a real turn in his work that no essay states outright.
- The three short 2025 notes (entropy-and-order, positive-sum-systems, meditations-on-community) are placeholders; he may want them rewritten or retired.
